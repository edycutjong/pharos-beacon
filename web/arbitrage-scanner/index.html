Arbitrage Scanner

Is the price gap between two real pools worth taking? One scan reads a Uniswap-V3-style pool and a FaroSwap pool at the same block, prices every round trip at several sizes, takes off swap fees, price impact, a slippage allowance and gas, and ACTs only above your profit threshold, otherwise it says which cost ate the spread. Mainnet is never signed: an ACT is an unsigned plan.

      What's ours

      Beyond the requirement list

      Costs you can add up

      Fees, price impact, slippage allowance and gas are separate columns that sum to the net, from the pools' own quotes.

      A real market, honestly

      The backtest of the same rule over 24 real hours: the gross gap was positive in 25 of 25 scans, and the costs ate it every time. The scanner skips, and says so.

      Safe execution

      On Atlantic the trade runs with a minimum out on both legs and a fresh read of leg 2 with what leg 1 really paid; if the round trip no longer clears, it stops and says it holds the token.

      How this was built
      Built with Claude as a coding assistant. The design, the failure cases and the extra feature were chosen by me; the commit history shows the iterations.
