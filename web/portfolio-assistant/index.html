Portfolio Assistant

Ask a wallet a plain question: what do I hold, where is my USDC, what am I earning, do I owe anything. In English or Bahasa Indonesia. Rules classify the question (no LLM), the answer reads RockawayX, Centrifuge and Morpho Blue through the Chain MCP, and every figure carries an [F…] id that names the contract, the call and the block. What it cannot read, it says.
