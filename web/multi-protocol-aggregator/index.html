Multi-Protocol Aggregator

One interface over three protocols and six venues on Pharos mainnet. Ask for rates or positions, or ask where 250 USDC should go: it routes the request to the best venue, says why with the numbers, prepares the deposit or withdrawal as unsigned transactions, and keeps answering when a venue is down. Pick a venue to simulate as down and the others are still routed (a SIMULATED drill, labelled as one).
