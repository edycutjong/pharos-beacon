Liquidation Watchdog

Who is next to be liquidated? The watchdog reads real Morpho Blue borrowers on Pharos mainnet through the Chain MCP, computes each health factor with Morpho's own rule, ranks them closest-to-the-edge first, and every alert says how much to repay or add to get back to the warn line. A what-if cuts the collateral price and shows who crosses the line.

      What's ours

      Beyond the requirement list

      Morpho's own rule

      Health is collateral × oracle price × LLTV ÷ debt in integer maths, interest accrued by the market's own rate, so the number matches what the contract would decide.

      A what-if, not a guess

      Cut the collateral price by up to 50 % and watch positions cross from ok to warn to critical to liquidatable, on the same read.

      The fix, named

      Each alert ends with the repay amount or the extra collateral that restores the warn line, computed at the shocked price when a shock is set.
