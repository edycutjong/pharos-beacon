Gas Fee Optimizer

Tracks the Atlantic gas price, holds a transaction until gas is at or below your target or the deadline arrives, and reports what was paid against the price when you asked. At the deadline it sends whatever the price.

Set your own target and deadline
Sequence · Hold for 0.5 gwei · 30 s deadlineidle · press Run live

    One lamp per real gas reading, every 3 s. The hold ends at the target or at the send-by time (deadline − 8 s lead).

      What's ours

      Beyond the requirement list

      Percentile target

      --target-percentile 25 --window 20 means “send when gas is in the cheapest quarter of the last 20 samples”, so nobody has to guess a price. It waits for 5 samples first (one sample is always its own p25). Press “Cheapest quarter (p25)” to watch it warm up.

      A lead before the deadline

      Nonce, estimate, broadcast and the block take seconds, so the hold ends 8 s before the deadline (half of a short one). The run without a lead landed 1 s late; with the lead it was mined 7 s early. Both are in the recorded runs.

      A clock that cannot slip

      The loop checks the clock before and after every read, cuts a read that hangs at the deadline, and counts failed reads without stopping the clock. Tests with an injected clock cover a price that never drops and a read that never answers.

      How this was built
      Built with Claude as a coding assistant. The design, the failure cases and the extra feature were chosen by me; the commit history shows the iterations.
