Whale-Watching Agent

Watches seven addresses on Pharos mainnet and reports every USDC or WPROS move at or above a threshold: what moved, how much, from whom to whom and why it matters. Then one summary across all of them.

Reads only, through the hosted Pharos Chain MCP (keyless). Nothing is signed or sent. Thresholds start at the committed watchlist's values; change them and run again.

      What's ours

      Beyond the requirement list

      Discord alerts, never twice

      With --notify discord the CLI posts the moves furthest above their thresholds (ranked by multiple of the threshold, because 6- and 18-decimal amounts are not comparable). A sent-set makes a re-run send nothing twice: the recorded run delivered 3 messages, the re-run 0 (listed in section 02).

      Balances you can trust

      Every before/after balance is an archive eth_call read twice (a third time if they differ); two of three must agree or the report fails instead of printing a number. When a balance moved by more than this one transfer in the block, the report says so.

      Our own watchlist

      The seven addresses are not someone else's list: scripts/find-whales.ts ranked the top USDC and WPROS movers over 40,000 blocks. They are named watch-1 … watch-7 because we do not know who they are, and unknown counterparts print as “unlabelled wallet/contract”, never a guessed name.

      How this was built
      Built with Claude as a coding assistant. The design, the failure cases and the extra feature were chosen by me; the commit history shows the iterations.
