Scope: every account that appeared as `onBehalf` (topic 3) in a Morpho Blue `Supply(bytes32,address,address,uint256,uint256)` event
(0x18573fA18fd17dDfD790B4a5B5b2977aad3b4Efb) over the last ~3.2 million blocks before block 21,151,926 (about 34 days): blocks
17,951,926 to 20,751,926 in 400,000-block chunks (the lines starting with "range" below), plus the most recent 400,000 blocks in a
first pass (9 Supply logs, 2 accounts: 0x6Ea606…9071 and 0xBa3b26…b68a). For each account the scan then read position(id, account) in the six
Morpho markets the Liquidation Watchdog knows and balanceOf(account) of the RockawayX, JTRSY and HYB share tokens. It does NOT prove no other
address holds two protocols: it covers only suppliers seen in that window, and the RockawayX / fund wallets of the earlier tasks came from
tasks/portfolio-assistant/proof/wallet-search.txt (a different scan).
