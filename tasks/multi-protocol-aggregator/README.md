# Multi-Protocol Aggregator — Hard

**Task:** [Multi-Protocol Aggregator](https://pharos-agent-arena.vercel.app/tasks/multi-protocol-aggregator) (Hard · 200 pts): a Harbor-style agent extended across more than one protocol or vault: one interface to query and act across several protocols, picking the best venue for each request.

**What is real.** Everything runs on **Pharos mainnet (chain 1672)** through the Pharos Chain MCP, on three protocols behind one interface: the [RockawayX USDC vault](https://www.pharosscan.xyz/address/0x047cd0a91e9b92ed979189a6c8a120bf280f02e5) (ERC-4626), three [Morpho Blue](https://www.pharosscan.xyz/address/0x18573fA18fd17dDfD790B4a5B5b2977aad3b4Efb) USDC markets, and the JTRSY and HYB fund vaults (Centrifuge, ERC-7540). The wallets in the proofs are real addresses found on chain, none of them ours: a RockawayX holder (`0x62A0…0673`) and a JTRSY holder (`0x0665…4bE3`, a smart-contract wallet) from the Portfolio Assistant's scan, a contract that supplies a Morpho market (`0xBa3b…b68a`), and the burn address `0x…dEaD`, which holds positions in RockawayX and in three Morpho markets (about $1 each in RockawayX, APT/USDC and APC3M/USDC, $0.001 in syzUSD/USDC) and is the only address with positions in two protocols that [our scan](proof/wallet-search.txt) found (it covers Morpho suppliers of the last ~34 days, not every address). Nothing is mocked and nothing is signed or sent: an action comes out as **unsigned transactions** for the owner's wallet. Harbor/PharosPort is not integrated; the "Harbor-style" part is the interface: one request, many protocols, a chosen venue and a stated reason.

**Keys:** none. The shared settings loader (`src/lib/env.ts`) parses `~/.config/pharos/testnet.env` and the repo `.env` when the Chain MCP client is imported (for the server URL); nothing on this path calls `secret()` or opens a key, and the "no key read" in the output means no key is used. `src/aggregator/` and `index.ts` import no signer, ask for no key and cannot send (a test greps for it). Before a plan is shown, the aggregator decodes its own calldata the way a wallet would and refuses a plan that pays anyone but the owner, names another venue or Morpho market than its leg, or moves another amount or number of shares than its leg states.

**Try it** (keyless, read-only, mainnet; `--block` pins the reads so the rates, fees and balances reproduce the proofs; the gas lines follow the head; a run can take several minutes at the hosted server's rate limit):

```text
npm ci
npm run multi-protocol-aggregator -- deposit  --wallet 0x0665FDe254598e307b63f3aAe3cCd881a62d4bE3 --amount 250 --block 21146194
npm run multi-protocol-aggregator -- withdraw --wallet 0x62A0e4b7fD967fab5be1a733c09b22eBC4510673 --amount all --down morpho --block 21146194
```

**Run:** `npm run multi-protocol-aggregator -- positions|rates|deposit|withdraw --wallet 0x… [--amount 250|all] [--hold-days 30] [--windows 1d,7d,30d] [--haircut 0.5] [--down morpho|rockawayx|centrifuge|<venue id>,…] [--timeout-ms 120000] [--assume-funded] [--block N] [--native-usd N] [--plan file.json] [--proof name]`
- `positions`: what the wallet holds in each venue. `rates`: every venue's rate, fees and limits. `deposit` / `withdraw`: route the request, explain the choice, prepare the unsigned transactions (`--plan file.json` writes them).
- `--down`: an **outage drill** (protocol or one venue id such as `morpho-c79579`): that part is not contacted and every line that names the failed venue says SIMULATED (the summary lines `PARTLY UP`, `positions … UNKNOWN` and the note that `all` covers only the venues that answered do not, so look for `SIMULATED unavailable:` / `SIMULATED OUTAGE:` to tell a drill from a real failure). A real failure (RPC error, revert, no answer within `--timeout-ms`) is handled the same way without the flag.
- A deposit is refused if the wallet does not hold the USDC (`--assume-funded` prepares the plan anyway and says so).

**Uses:** Pharos Chain MCP `eth_call`, `get_block`, `get_block_number`, `get_gas_price`, `estimate_gas`, and `get_chain_info` (the client's chain-id check at connect) (hosted `https://mcp.pharos.xyz/mcp`). Architecture write-up: [`ARCHITECTURE.md`](ARCHITECTURE.md).

## How a request is routed

Each protocol is an `Adapter` (read a position, read the market, prepare a deposit, prepare a withdrawal); the router sees only venues and their readings. Full picture in [`ARCHITECTURE.md`](ARCHITECTURE.md).

- **Rates** are share-price growth over 1d/7d/30d, annualised (simple); a venue's *conservative* rate is the median minus `haircut × spread`, so a jumpy rate is ranked down (a withdrawal gives up the *optimistic* rate, median plus `haircut × spread`; the same readers as the [Yield Optimizer](../yield-optimizer/), including its previous-block confirmation of every share price).
- **Deposit:** the venue with the best `yield − entry fee − gas` per dollar placed over `--hold-days`, with Morpho's rate diluted by our own deposit. A venue that cannot take it (asynchronous deposit, `maxDeposit` 0, unreadable rate or fee) is listed with the reason, never silently dropped. If the best venue's limit is below the amount, the rest goes to the next best.
- **Withdraw:** the venue where leaving costs least per dollar goes first: `exit fee + gas + the yield given up`, so the best-paying positions are touched last. What can leave is limited by the position and, on Morpho, by the market's idle liquidity; vault exits are simulated with `eth_call` from the wallet.
- **Explanation:** the chosen venue with its numbers, the runner-up and the gap, every ineligible venue with its reason, every unavailable venue.

## Limits, stated plainly

- **The wallet-side check is a self-consistency check:** it decodes the calldata against the plan's own legs, which catches an adapter bug or edited calldata; a real wallet must also check the legs against its own list of known venues and markets (a plan edited consistently in both places would pass). The plans for the burn address are routing demonstrations on dust: no one is known to hold a key for it (a conventional burn address).
- **Nothing is executed.** Every action is a prepared plan; no transaction was sent for this task. The wallet-side check is our own decoder, not a hardware wallet's.
- **Routing is yield and cost, not risk.** A Morpho market's collateral risk, a fund's NAV risk or a vault's strategy risk is not scored. Rates are backward-looking share-price growth, not forecasts.
- **RockawayX is not chosen for a deposit today:** its `maxDeposit(wallet)` is 0 for both wallets in the deposit and rates proofs (the entry lines of [`mainnet-rates.txt`](proof/mainnet-rates.txt) and [`mainnet-deposit-250.txt`](proof/mainnet-deposit-250.txt)), so the output lists it as not eligible with that reason. We do not simulate a deposit (it needs an approve first), so the vault may accept deposits its `maxDeposit` does not report: its `maxRedeem` is 0 too, yet a simulated redeem of all shares succeeds ([`mainnet-withdraw-5000.json`](proof/mainnet-withdraw-5000.json), the `simulated redeem` evidence). We report the reason instead of calling the vault closed.
- **Centrifuge funds are read, compared and shown among the positions, but never routed to:** deposits and redeems are asynchronous requests (ERC-7540) settled in a later epoch for approved investors, so no one-transaction plan exists. A withdrawal request for a wallet that holds only those says so.
- **Cross-protocol withdrawals are shown live only on dust** (the burn address's positions, about $1 each in three venues and $0.001 in a fourth); the larger live withdrawals have one eligible venue each. The live outage proofs are drills (`--down`); the failure paths of a real outage are tested.
- **Routing does not split a deposit to reduce Morpho dilution:** a big deposit goes to the single best market (and dilutes it) unless a `maxDeposit` limit forces a split. `withdraw --amount all` on a Morpho position larger than the market's idle liquidity takes what can leave and says how much stays.
- **Gas:** routing weighs gas at configured defaults (a deposit 60,000 + 300,000 units, a withdrawal 250,000); the plan's transactions show `estimate_gas` from the owner's address (labelled "estimated"); a deposit step always shows the configured 300,000 (it cannot be estimated before the approve is mined), and a step whose estimate fails shows its configured default, also labelled "configured". The node's gas price follows the head even with `--block`.
- **A pinned block does not make the hosted node deterministic:** it has been seen to serve slightly different historical values for the same block (see [`node-flake.txt`](../yield-optimizer/proof/node-flake.txt)), so a re-run can differ in the second decimal of a Morpho rate; the previous-block confirmation of every price narrows this, not removes it.
- **Exit fees are not grossed up:** a partial vault withdrawal redeems the shares worth the amount before any exit fee (all current exit fees are 0 bps). Venues are read one after another, so a hung venue costs `--timeout-ms` (default 120 s) before the next is read.
- **Sources are a fixed list:** RockawayX, the JTRSY and HYB funds, and the Morpho USDC markets found by the Liquidation Watchdog's scan (three that lend USDC and hold at least 1,000 USDC; the report lists the others as left out).
- **Harbor / PharosPort** is not integrated.
